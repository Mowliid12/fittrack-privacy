Account Access

I can't sign in

FitTrack supports signing in with email and password, Google, or Apple. If you signed up with one method, you'll need to use that same method to sign back in — accounts aren't automatically linked across different sign-in methods.

If you use email and password and forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password?" link on the sign-in screen to reset it.

My Google or Apple sign-in isn't working

Make sure you have a working internet connection and that you're selecting the same Google or Apple account you originally signed up with. If it still doesn't work, contact us below with your device type (iPhone or Android) and we'll help.

Workouts

How do workouts work?

Choose Home Workout (no equipment, free) or Gym Workout (full equipment, included with FitTrack Premium) from the Workout tab. Your completed sets and sessions are saved automatically to your account as you go.

Where is my workout history?

Open the Workout tab and tap "Recent Workouts," or check the Progress tab for your workout consistency and streak.

FitTrack Premium (Subscriptions)

What does Premium include?

FitTrack Premium unlocks Gym Workouts (full equipment training) and Progress Photos (uploading new photos and using Before/After comparison). Everything else in FitTrack remains free.

How is Premium billed?

FitTrack Premium is a Monthly or Yearly auto-renewing subscription, billed through your Apple ID (iOS) or Google Play account (Android) — the same way all App Store and Play Store subscriptions work. Pricing is shown in the app before you purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions) — not inside FitTrack directly, since Apple and Google handle billing for all apps this way.

Restore Purchases

What does "Restore Purchases" actually do?

If you've already purchased FitTrack Premium — for example, after reinstalling the app or signing in on a new device — "Restore Purchases" reconnects your existing subscription to your FitTrack account. It only works if you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account originally used for the purchase.

Tapping Restore Purchases does not itself create or activate a new subscription — it only looks for one that already exists on your account. If there's nothing to restore, nothing will be unlocked. It can also take a short moment after restoring for Premium features to unlock in the app — if that happens, try closing and reopening the app.

I restored, but Premium still isn't unlocked

First, confirm you're signed into FitTrack with the account that originally purchased Premium, and that you restored using the matching Apple ID or Google account. If you've checked that and it's still not working after a few minutes, contact us below and include your purchase date and payment receipt if you have one — this helps us look into it faster.

Account Deletion

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, measurements, workout history, nutrition entries, progress photos, and reminder preferences, and closes your sign-in. This action is permanent and cannot be undone from the app.

Does deleting my account cancel my subscription?

No — deleting your FitTrack account does not automatically cancel an active Apple or Google subscription. Please cancel your subscription separately through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings (see "FitTrack Premium" above) before or after deleting your account, to avoid being charged for a subscription you're no longer using.
